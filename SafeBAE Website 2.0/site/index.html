A SafeBAE campaign

WarningChoking is a hazardMay cause serious injury or deathchokinghazard.org

There’s no safe way to choke someone.

Choking Hazard is SafeBAE’s campaign on sexual strangulation, which young people call “choking.” It gives young people, parents and educators the evidence, myth vs. fact, signs that need medical care, and free warning posters.

About the campaign→chokinghazard.org ↗
Vibe Check by SafeBAE
Did I harm someone?Was that consent?Did I cross a boundary?Am I a bad person?Not sure if you crossed a line?

CheckYourVibe.org is a free, anonymous reflection tool for young people who are questioning whether they, or a friend, may have crossed a boundary or caused harm.

Young people are increasingly turning to anonymous forums and AI chatbots to ask difficult questions about relationships and consent. AI is built to tell you what you want to hear. Vibe Check guides users through confusing situations and connects them with real, evidence-based resources on consent, communication and accountability. No names, no accounts, and nothing is stored.

#ForDaisy

Remembering our co-founder, Daisy Coleman.

As a teenage survivor, Daisy shared her story in the documentary Audrie & Daisy. Her vision became the foundation of SafeBAE and helped ignite a youth-led movement. Every year, hundreds of people still find their way to this work through her story.

She was also a tattoo artist whose work people still carry with them.

All the organizing that happened after Daisy spoke out may not necessarily be a direct result but is certainly a ripple effect. The Cut, “What Daisy Coleman Changed”
