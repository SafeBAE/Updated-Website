Chip in

Did you find SafeBAE’s resources helpful?

We believe every young person deserves access to clear, honest, and practical information about consent, relationships, and safety, without barriers. If something you found here helped you, you can help us keep these resources free and accessible to young people everywhere.

Your support helps make this possible
  • Trained 3,000+ young people as Certified Peer Educators
  • Built a growing library of consent and healthy relationship resources used by students and educators nationwide
  • Delivered workshops, classroom sessions, and peer-led trainings across the country
  • Created youth-led media and tools that reach young people where they are, online and in their communities

Everything we create is led by young people, and kept free for youth so that everyone can access it when they need it.
