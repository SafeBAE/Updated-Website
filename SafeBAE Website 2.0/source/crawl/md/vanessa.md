## Vanessa's SafeBAE Fundraiser!

This month, I’m using my platform to raise awareness and funds for SafeBAE, an organization I wish was around when I was a teenager. It is a survivor-founded, youth-led organization dedicated to ending sexual violence and supporting mental health among middle and high school students.

You can also support this fundraiser via Venmo. To help us properly track donations and giveaway entries, please include **your instagram handle (username)** in the payment note.
