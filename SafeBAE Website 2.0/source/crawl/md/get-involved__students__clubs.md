## Start a SafeBAE club at your school

A SafeBAE club is a student-run chapter that brings consent and healthy relationship education to your school. You decide what your chapter does. We provide the training and materials, and our team is a quick email away when you need help. Registration is free.

[Register Your Club](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfoQAsZ5bZpRyBpL1ELxoC0yk5ewm5mkpoz6wwR49IcGoKtLQ/viewform)
[Already Registered? Club Hub](https://clubs.safebae.org)

### Three ways a club joins

Whether you’re starting from scratch, restarting a previous chapter, or adding SafeBAE to a club you already run, the Club Hub walks you through it step by step. All three register through the same form and get the same Hub access and support.

**Start a new chapter**
*Bring SafeBAE to your school for the first time*
Start a SafeBAE chapter from the ground up. Find an advisor, register your club, recruit your team, and use the Club Hub to plan meetings, activities, and events throughout the year.

**Affiliate an existing club**
*Bring SafeBAE into a club that’s already active*
Add SafeBAE to the work your club is already doing. Register as an affiliate, get access to the Club Hub, and use our activities, campaigns, and resources as part of your existing programming.

**Restart a previous chapter**
*Bring your school’s SafeBAE chapter back*
Pick up where a previous SafeBAE chapter left off. Re-register your chapter, reconnect with an advisor and students, and use the Club Hub to get meetings, activities, and events going again.

Members Only

### Registering unlocks the Club Hub

The Club Hub is the members-only site for registered SafeBAE chapters. Access is free and renews with your registration each school year. Inside, you’ll find:

* **Find Your Next Action** to figure out what your chapter should do next
* **Awareness Event Library** and tools for planning your year
* **Step-By-Step Guides** for running meetings and organizing events
* **Prevention Poster Builder** for creating posters with your school’s support resources
* **Guides for Running Your Chapter**, from finding an advisor and recruiting members to getting media attention and preparing the next group of leaders
* **And More!** New tools, activities, and resources are added to the Club Hub throughout the year.

[Register to Get Access](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfoQAsZ5bZpRyBpL1ELxoC0yk5ewm5mkpoz6wwR49IcGoKtLQ/viewform)

### Help from our team

Email clubsupport@safebae.org or book a virtual meeting with our programming team. Students and faculty advisors can both reach out with questions, challenges, or event ideas.

After an event, send us photos and a couple of sentences. We share chapters’ work with clubs at other schools and on our social media, and we credit your club and school.

[Email the Team](mailto:clubsupport@safebae.org)
[Book a Meeting](https://calendly.com/clubsupport-safebae/30min)

### Registration takes a few minutes

It’s free and renews each school year. Once you register, you’ll get access to the Club Hub and be connected with your point of contact on the SafeBAE team.

[Register Your Club](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfoQAsZ5bZpRyBpL1ELxoC0yk5ewm5mkpoz6wwR49IcGoKtLQ/viewform)
[Already Registered? Club Hub](https://clubs.safebae.org)

Joining a club that’s already registered? You don’t need to register the club again — just [create your own Club Hub account](https://clubs.safebae.org). Need help? Email [clubsupport@safebae.org](mailto:clubsupport@safebae.org).
