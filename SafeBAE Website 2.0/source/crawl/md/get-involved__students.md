# For Students

#### SafeBAE was founded by teen survivors who wanted their schools to change. Everything on this site was built by students for you. You can become a Certified Peer Educator, start a SafeBAE club at your school, push your district to fix its policies, or find information and support for yourself or a friend. Wherever you start, you are part of a national movement of students working to end sexual violence in schools.

### Become a Certified Peer Educator

A free, self-paced training for students who want to lead prevention at their school. Sixteen online modules covering healthy relationships and boundaries, consent, bystander intervention, online safety, student rights under Title IX, how to support a survivor, and more!

* Free, self-paced, and open to high school students anywhere
* Support from our team and a community of other peer educators
* Finish the modules and the after survey to earn your certificate

[Get Registered](https://www.safebae.org/get-involved/students/become-a-peer-educator/studentregistration/)
[Resume Training](https://learning.safebae.org/students)

### Explore SafeBAE Clubs

Starting a SafeBAE club focused on consent and dating violence prevention can help shift the culture among your peers and build a safer school community. You can start a new club or integrate SafeBAE programming into a group you already belong to.

* Free to register, at any school, with or without an existing club
* Members-only Club Hub: tools, activities, event planning
* Virtual training sessions with the SafeBAE team

Start Here · Three ways to do it

**Start a New Chapter**
If there’s nothing like this at your school yet

**Affiliate a Club You Already Run**
Student council, a wellness club, an affinity group

**Restart Last Year’s Chapter**
If your school used to have one

[Learn More](https://www.safebae.org/get-involved/students/clubs)

### Vibe Check

A private, self-guided reflection tool for anyone questioning whether they, or a friend, may have crossed a line. Nothing is ever reported and no account is required. It walks you through what happened and what to do next.

[Vibe Check](https://checkyourvibe.org)

### BAE Skills Lab

Short interactive activities about consent, boundaries, and healthy relationships. Explore the BAE Skills Lab to put your learning into practice and strengthen your skills as a peer educator and club leader.

[Skills Lab](https://skills-lab.safebae.org/)

### Title IX & Sexual Misconduct Policy

Title IX is the federal law that bars sex discrimination, including sexual harassment and assault, at any school that takes federal funding.

#### Know your rights

Your right to report, to supportive measures like a schedule change, and to a prompt, fair investigation.

#### Find your coordinator

Learn whether your school is covered by Title IX, and how to find your school's Title IX Coordinator.

#### Understand and change your school's policy

How to understand your school's sexual misconduct policy, what a strong one should include, and how to advocate for change.

[Learn More](https://www.safebae.org/resources/title-ix/)
[Policy Reform Guide](https://www.safebae.org/get-involved/students/reform_school_policy/)
