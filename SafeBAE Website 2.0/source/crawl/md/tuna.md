## Tina's SafeBAE Fundraiser!

For tonight’s livestream I’m using my platform to raise awareness and funds for SafeBAE, an organization I wish was around when I was a teenager. It is a survivor-founded, youth-led organization dedicated to ending sexual assault and dating violence among middle and high school students.

You can also support this fundraiser via Venmo. To help us properly track donations and giveaway entries, please include **TUNA** in the payment note.

[Venmo @Safe-BAE](https://venmo.com/safe-bae "Venmo")
