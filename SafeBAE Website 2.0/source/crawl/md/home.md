#### Introducing Vibe Check

## **CheckYourVibe.org**

SafeBAE has launched [CheckYourVibe.org](http://checkyourvibe.org), a free, anonymous reflection tool designed for young people who are questioning whether they may have crossed a boundary or caused harm.

Young people are increasingly turning to anonymous forums and AI chatbots to ask difficult questions about relationships and consent. But AI is designed to tell you what you want to hear — not help you reflect or take accountability. Vibe Check guides users through confusing situations and connects them with real, evidence-based resources on consent, communication, and accountability — not AI advice.

### For Daisy: Before Anyone Else

## Certified Peer Educator Training

SafeBAE’s Certified Peer Educator Training empowers young people to lead conversations about consent, healthy relationships, and their lived experiences by equipping them with the knowledge, confidence, and leadership skills to drive prevention efforts in their own schools and communities. #### YOUTH-LED, SCHOOL-WIDE CULTURE CHANGE

## **SafeBAE 360º Schools**

SafeBAE’s 360° is a comprehensive, youth-led prevention model that transforms school culture around consent, relationships, and safety. Rooted in trauma-informed care and evidence-based practices, it empowers students, educators, and administrators to drive meaningful, lasting change. Schools can engage with one or more core components — Certified Peer Educator Training, a 16-lesson healthy relationship and consent education curriculum, faculty and staff training, and sexual misconduct policy consultation — each contributing to safer, more supportive learning environments.

[LEARN MORE](https://www.safebae.org/360-schools/360-schools/ "SafeBAE 360 Schools")

### Document your experience — privately and securely

SafeBAE is partnering with REES to offer a free tool that gives every student a private, encrypted space to document incidents of harm or violence at their own pace. Nothing is shared without your consent.
