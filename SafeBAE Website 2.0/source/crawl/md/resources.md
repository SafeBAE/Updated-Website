## SafeBAE Resources

All of SafeBAE’s resources are free for students and created by young people, with support from adult allies and professionals. Explore tools for learning about consent and healthy relationships, finding support, building skills, and creating change in your school or community. Start with one of our featured resources below, or browse by who you are and what you need.

For student leaders

### Certified Peer Educator Training

A free, self-paced 16-module training that prepares students to lead sexual violence prevention in their schools and communities.

[Explore the training →](https://www.safebae.org/resources/peer-educator-training/)

Reflection & accountability

### Vibe Check

A free and anonymous tool for young people who are worried they may have crossed a boundary or caused harm.

[Visit CheckYourVibe.org ↗](https://checkyourvibe.org/)

Student-led action

### SafeBAE Clubs

Start or lead a student chapter and get access to materials, training, event ideas, direct support, and a national network of student leaders.

[Explore SafeBAE Clubs →](https://www.safebae.org/get-involved/students/clubs/)

Films + ready-to-use lessons

### Video Library & Teaching Guides

Pair youth-created films with activities, discussion prompts, and facilitation guidance for classrooms, clubs, and community programs.

[Watch films →](https://www.safebae.org/resources/videos/)
[Use teaching guides →](https://www.safebae.org/resources/teaching-guides/)

Games + practice

### Skills Lab

Interactive activities about consent, boundaries, and what a healthy relationship actually looks like.

[Explore the Skills Lab ↗](https://skills-lab.safebae.org/)
