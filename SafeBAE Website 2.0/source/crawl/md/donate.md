# Donate to SafeBAE

Safe Before Anyone Else

## Help end sexual violence before it starts.

SafeBAE is a survivor-founded, youth-led organization dedicated to ending sexual violence before it starts. Our programs are free for students and designed to create lasting culture change in schools, backed by a growing body of evidence showing real impact.

Born from lived experience, SafeBAE began when our co-founders met during the filming of Netflix’s *Audrie & Daisy*, united by a shared goal: to prevent what happened to us from happening to anyone else. Today, we’re a national organization empowering young people to lead the work of sexual violence prevention.

Your gift funds

* Peer educator training for student leaders across the country
* 360° Schools, our whole-school prevention program
* Free tools like Vibe Check that help teens reflect on their own behavior
* Survivor support and trauma-informed policy advocacy

Monthly gifts help most. They give us steady funding we can plan around.

SafeBAE is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit with a Candid Platinum Seal of Transparency. Gifts are tax-deductible. EIN 81‑5451147.
