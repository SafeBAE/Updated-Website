# Contact Us

### Whether you are a student, educator, parent, journalist, or potential partner, here is how to reach us. We look forward to hearing from you!

**General Inquiries** Email [info@safebae.org](mailto:info@safebae.org) or use the form on this page. We respond within 2 to 3 business days.

**Press and Media** Email [info@safebae.org](mailto:info@safebae.org) with your outlet, topic, and deadline, or use the form. If you are on deadline, you can reach Shael Norris, Executive Director ([shael@safebae.org](mailto:shael@safebae.org)), or Drew Davis ([drew@safebae.org](mailto:drew@safebae.org)), directly.

**Partnerships** We partner with schools, districts, nonprofits, brands, and funders. Email [info@safebae.org](mailto:info@safebae.org) with a short description of your organization and what you have in mind.

**Follow Us** New resources, campaigns, and youth organizing updates on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/safe_bae/), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@safe_bae), and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/safebae/).
