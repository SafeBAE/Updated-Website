# Professional Development for Faculty and Staff

Prevention is most effective when faculty and staff are empowered with the right tools and training. SafeBAE provides **professional development for teachers, staff, and administrators** that is trauma-informed, flexible, and tailored to your school’s unique needs.

Our trainings focus on:

* Trauma-informed responses to disclosures of harm
* Building a school culture of consent and accountability
* Supporting student survivors
* Understanding and advancing [school policy reform](https://www.safebae.org/360-schools/school-policy-reform-guide/) to strengthen protections and reduce liability
* Supporting youth leadership and engagement in prevention

We adapt every training to the context of your school, and sessions can be delivered **in-person or virtually**. Ready to take the next step? Click the button below to request professional development training for your school.
