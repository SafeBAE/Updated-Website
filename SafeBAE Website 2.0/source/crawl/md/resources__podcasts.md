# SafeBAE's Podcast Library

SafeBAE’s founders, staff, and student leaders have joined podcasts hosted by journalists, educators, researchers, and prevention professionals to talk about preventing sexual assault and dating violence among teens. These are conversations we were invited into, and each was recorded for a different audience: parents, educators, prevention professionals, survivors, and young people themselves. The topics range from our founding story and school-based prevention model to teen dating violence, the manosphere, and [Vibe Check](https://www.checkyourvibe.org). Search by keyword, filter by topic, or sort by date. Every episode is free to listen to and free to share.

**Interested in featuring SafeBAE on your show or in your reporting? Email us at [info@safebae.org](mailto:info@safebae.org).**
