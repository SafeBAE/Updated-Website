# Partners & Press

SafeBAE’s work is made possible by the incredible partnerships we’ve built with schools, researchers, funders, creatives, and youth-serving organizations across the country. We’re proud to collaborate with those who share our commitment to ending sexual violence, amplifying youth leadership, and shifting culture through education, art, media, and policy. Below, you’ll find a selection of our collaborators, recent media features, and opportunities to partner with us.

### SafeBAE on The Longest Shortest Time

#### **“Preventing Teens from Joining the Manosphere”**

SafeBAE returned to **[The Longest Shortest Time](https://longestshortesttime.com/episode-255-preventing-teens-from-joining-the-manosphere)** for a conversation with Hillary Frank about [**Vibe Check**](https://checkyourvibe.org/), SafeBAE’s free, anonymous reflection tool for young people who are worried they, or a friend, may have crossed a boundary or caused harm. Featuring Drew Davis, SafeBAE’s Director of Strategic Initiatives, the episode explores what happens when teens bring sensitive questions to Reddit, AI tools, or online spaces that can reinforce shame, denial, misogyny, or defensiveness — and how Vibe Check offers a different path toward reflection, accountability, and repair.

#### “Centering Youth Voices: Youth-Led Prevention and Addressing Harm”

SafeBAE Founding Executive Director Shael Norris joined **[The She Matters Podcast](https://www.shematters.ca/podcasts/the-she-matters-podcast/episodes/2149218219)** to discuss youth-led sexual violence prevention, SafeBAE’s founding story, and what it means to meaningfully center young people in this work. The episode also explores SafeBAE’s school-based prevention model, [Certified Peer Educator Training](https://www.safebae.org/resources/peer-educator-training/), and [Vibe Check](https://checkyourvibe.org/), our accountability tool for young people who may have caused harm.

#### Shaping the National Conversation with The Longest Shortest Time

## **“How to Not (Accidentally) Raise a Rapist”**

The first episode of the relaunch of Hilary Frank’s parenting and culture podcast, reframing one of her most downloaded episodes ([*How to Not (Accidentally) Raise a Racist*](https://longestshortesttime.com/episode-116-how-to-not-accidentally-raise-a-racist/)) to spark dialogue about raising young people who respect boundaries and safety. Featuring SafeBAE’s work and Executive Director Shael Norris, the episode explores what actually works when talking to teens about consent and how schools and parents can build a consent-conscious culture.

SafeBAE’s peer-led prevention model was also featured in Hilary Frank’s **New York Times Opinion** piece [*“Our Kids Cannot Learn About Sex Just From Squeamish Gym Teachers”*](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/01/26/opinion/sex-education-consent-middle-school.html) and the accompanying podcast *“[The Most Important Lesson in Sex Ed Isn’t Actually Sex,”](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/02/27/opinion/sex-education-consent.html)* which highlight why students are the most effective messengers for prevention.

#### “Reducing Tolerance for Sexual Assault”

SafeBAE appeared on *The Measure of Everyday Life* with host Dr. Brian Southwell, discussing the findings of our [published research paper](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10538712.2024.2385468) — and announcing the launch of SafeBAE’s newest resource, [*CheckYourVibe.org*](http://checkyourvibe.org) — a free, anonymous reflection tool designed for young people who are questioning whether they may have caused harm.

### The Totally Preventable Podcast

SafeBAE was featured on [*The Totally Preventable Podcast*](https://strategicprevention.org/totally-preventable/) in the two-part episode *“Blurred Lines,”* a conversation exploring the intersection of alcohol, drugs, and sexual assault alongside experts from RAINN, DanceSafe, Day One, and more. The podcast highlights local voices and prevention leaders working to address behavioral health and social issues across Newport County, offering insight, resources, and practical ways to support safer, healthier communities. Listen to the podcast episode today!

#### “Sexual Violence Prevention in Schools with SafeBAE”

Hosted by Freya Graf, this candid conversation explores building authentic, youth-driven prevention and meeting young people where they are. Featuring SafeBAE, the episode highlights how partnering with students creates systemic change in school environments and fosters cultures of consent.

#### Streaming Now

## Torrey DeVitto on *Celebrity Name That Tune*

Longtime SafeBAE partner and Board Member Torrey DeVitto recently competed on [*Celebrity Name That Tune*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XFQeOEDbN4M) (Season 5, Episode 12) on FOX — and chose SafeBAE as the beneficiary of her winnings. Torrey donated $29,000 to support youth-led sexual violence prevention, bringing SafeBAE’s mission to a national television audience. Her win reflects an ongoing commitment to our work and demonstrates the power of trusted advocates using their platforms to invest directly in prevention, care, and youth leadership. We’re deeply grateful to Torrey for continuing to show up for SafeBAE — both publicly and behind the scenes.

## Disfluency

Disfluency is an award-winning feature film written and directed by [**Anna Baumgarten**](https://www.annabaumgarten.com/) that offers a survivor-centered portrayal of the aftermath of sexual assault, focusing on trauma, memory, and recovery without depicting the assault itself. SafeBAE partnered with the filmmakers to support the film’s educational impact, collaborating on school-based screenings and accompanying prevention and discussion resources for secondary and higher-education settings.

## Training & Education for Professionals

SafeBAE’s commitment to prevention extends beyond students. We regularly work with educators, legal advocates, service providers, parents, caregivers, and community leaders to share our youth-led model and equip adults with the tools to support survivors and embed prevention into everyday practice. Through keynote presentations, workshops, and national webinars, we provide concrete strategies for engaging youth as leaders, building consent-conscious environments, and sustaining systemic change.
