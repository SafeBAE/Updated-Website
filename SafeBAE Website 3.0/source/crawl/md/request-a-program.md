# Request a Program

#### Bring SafeBAE to your school, campus or community.

#### Assemblies & One-Time Events

We know many school partners seek one-time assemblies or awareness events as an accessible first step — and we’re happy to offer them. While assemblies alone cannot replace sustained prevention work, they can spark critical conversations and open the door to deeper engagement.

Our assemblies and school events are always co-presented with youth leaders and tailored to be age-appropriate, relevant, and impactful. They are a powerful entry point for schools beginning their partnership with us, and often serve as the starting place for longer-term collaboration.

#### 360° Schools: A Comprehensive Prevention Model

For schools seeking more than one-time events, SafeBAE’s comprehensive 360° Schools program offers a flexible, youth-led approach to building safer school communities. The model includes five core components: peer educator training, a trauma-informed consent curriculum, faculty and staff professional development, policy support, and educational tools to reduce recidivism.

Each component can be delivered individually or be integrated as part of our whole-school approach, tailored to your school’s needs, goals, and capacity.
