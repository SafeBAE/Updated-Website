# School Sexual Misconduct Policy Reform Guide

## Help Reform Your School's Sexual Misconduct Policy

Strong, trauma-informed policies are the foundation of a safe school community. Yet since 2020, shifting federal Title IX regulations have left schools caught in the middle of inconsistent rules, legal challenges, and unclear enforcement. The 2020 rule narrowed protections for survivors and restricted schools’ ability to respond effectively. While 2024 guidelines attempted to restore protections, they were quickly tied up in court — forcing most schools back under the restrictive 2020 rule.

This federal uncertainty leaves both students and school districts vulnerable: survivors face heightened risk of further harm, and administrators face potential legal exposure without clear, stable standards to follow.

That’s why SafeBAE developed the **School Policy Reform Guide** — a step-by-step resource to help administrators, faculty, and district leaders strengthen their policies beyond the bare federal minimum. Grounded in trauma-informed practice and survivor-centered values, this guide walks you through building policies that promote transparency, accountability, and safety for every student.

By proactively strengthening your school’s policies, you not only protect students — you also safeguard your institution and demonstrate leadership in creating safer, healthier learning environments.
