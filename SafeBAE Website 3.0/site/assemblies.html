Assemblies & one-time events

Many school partners start with a one-time assembly or awareness event. Ours are always co-presented with youth leaders and tailored to your students.

Request an assembly→Book a meeting ↗
Co-presented with youth leaders

An assembly is a first step

We know many school partners seek one-time assemblies or awareness events as an accessible first step, and we’re happy to offer them.

  1. An accessible first stepOne assembly or awareness event
  2. It can sparkCritical conversations
  3. It can open the door toDeeper engagement
  4. It is often the starting place forLonger-term collaboration

Assemblies alone cannot replace sustained prevention work.

They are a powerful entry point for schools beginning their partnership with us.

Where schools go after an assembly

Some partners begin with a single assembly or awareness event, while others start a deeper collaboration right away. Wherever you begin, we can help you take actionable steps toward building safer, more supportive school environments.
