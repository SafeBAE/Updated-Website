# SafeBAE website 3.0

The third version of the rebuilt safebae.org: 49 pages. Versions 1 and 2 sit untouched next to this folder (`../site` and `../SafeBAE Website 2.0`).

## Open it

- **Quick look:** open `site/index.html` in Chrome or Safari. Pages, images, menus, search, tabs, players and interactives all work from your computer.
- **Embeds:** YouTube, Spotify, Zeffy, Google Forms and Calendly load only when the site is served from a real web address. Opened from your computer, those spots show a button that opens the same video, episode or form in a new tab.
- **Online preview:** the same build is published as a private Claude artifact, "SafeBAE Site 3.0". Links between pages swap the page in place there, so the browser Back button doesn't step between pages.

## What changed from 2.0

**Navigation: six tabs.** About · Students · Adults · Request a Program · Resources · #ForDaisy.

- **Request a Program** replaces 360° Schools as a tab. Under it: Request a Program (the hub, with every program and the request form), SafeBAE 360° Schools (framed as the whole-school approach behind the programs), Pricing, Assemblies, Consent Curriculum, Faculty & Staff Training, Policy Reform Guide, Accountability Training and Sexual Strangulation Prevention.
- **Resources** now holds what used to be the Library tab, plus the trainings and campaigns, in four groups: Trainings & tools (Peer Educator Training, AI Literacy Training, Vibe Check, BAE Skills Lab, En Español) · The Library (search, films, podcasts, guides, posters) · Campaigns & series (Choking Hazard, #KnowB4UNude, It's Giving Red Flags, BAEcode: Game On!, Race, Rape Culture & Resistance) · Support & answers (Survivor Support, Title IX, Myths & Facts, FAQ, Blog).
- Peer Educator Training is listed under Resources, and also under Students and #ForDaisy.
- **#ForDaisy** is still its own tab.
- Each tab opens a full-width menu with grouped links and one featured card. Breadcrumbs on every page follow the new structure.

**Two new pages**

| Page | File | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Assemblies | `assemblies.html` | The assemblies program: what an assembly is and is not, what every one includes, films to build an event around, what school leaders say, where schools go next, pricing factors and how to request one. |
| BAE Skills Lab | `skills-lab.html` | A landing page for skills-lab.safebae.org in the Lab's own look: the six tools with screenshots and times, a "how long do you have?" picker, running a tool with a group, and links to the Peer Educator Training and Vibe Check. |

**Footer.** The wordmark is two lines that fill the width, with SAFE and the B, A, E in solid neon mint. Under it: logo and mission, Donate and newsletter, social icons, six link columns, and the nonprofit line.

**The daisy.** The drawing you sent is traced into a vector (`assets/img/daisy-line.svg`) and replaces the old daisy in every hero and in the footer. It no longer rotates. The #ForDaisy pages, the Peer Educator dedication and the home page's #ForDaisy section use it as their main motif. The small daisy icon in the menu and buttons is unchanged, because the drawing turns to a smudge below about 30px.

**#KnowB4UNude** has its own design built from the campaign's materials: the lavender field, peach and eggplant emoji as large graphics, Polaroid frames, the block logo as the headline, and the posters' heavy serif.

**Request a Program** is now the hub for its tab: a menu of nine programs with one card layout, then a band on the 360° approach, then testimonial, pricing and the form.

**Layout pass on every page.** Rows of boxes share one grid and end level, card groups use one layout, short headings no longer sit alone beside long lists, photos fill the height of the text beside them, running text is 15px or larger, and nothing loops forever (marquees, rotating text and the slide player now play once and rest). Each page keeps one signature interactive or visual.

Other campaign and tool pages also moved further into their own look: It's Giving Red Flags (neon sign), BAEcode: Game On! (arcade cabinet and scoreboard), End Online Harassment of Survivors (the #QuitThisShit typewriter tiles), Vibe Check (its own lockup and ticks), and the #ForDaisy pages.

## Please check these before launch

**New in 3.0**

- **Assemblies page facts.** The current site says very little about assemblies, so the page uses only that text, the pricing factors, the school-leader testimonial and the list of films marked for assembly use. It has no topics, length, grade levels or prices. Add them if you want them.
- **Male survivors film page.** The old hero photo was not Theo (it was from the #QuitThisShit shoot), so the hero now uses his own video still.
- **Myths & Facts brick wall.** The "Reporting for survivors can feel like hitting a brick wall" graphic is now built in the page, with the same sentence, so it stays sharp at full height. It is no longer your image file.
- **Team photos.** Every portrait is re-cropped to a square headshot at one scale. Mikayla Murphy's and Len Eschweiler's source photos are low resolution and look slightly soft; so do two Summer Institute fellows' (Cole Firshein, Maria Muntean).
- **#ForDaisy photo description.** One photo was described as "Daisy at a tattoo studio". It shows Daisy on a photo set holding up her tattooed hand, so the description now says that.
- **Wording taken from the live site, not from 2.0:** the sentence "This federal uncertainty leaves both students and school districts vulnerable" on the Policy Reform Guide page, and an "81%" poster card on Faculty & Staff Training. Both are SafeBAE's own words.
- **Consent Curriculum.** There is no slide preview, because no curriculum slides exist in the site's images. Page 1 of the facilitator's guide stands in as the sample.
- **Donation forms.** The Zeffy frames on Donate, Chip In and Fundraise are set taller (1040, 1100 and 960px) so the columns beside them end level. Look at the three pages once on a live address; if the real forms are much shorter there will be white space inside the frame.
- **Emoji on #KnowB4UNude** are real emoji characters, so they look like Apple's on a Mac or iPhone and like each system's own elsewhere. Worth one look on your Mac.
- **Press logos.** Partners & Press uses the New York Times nameplate (from Wikimedia Commons) and square podcast covers from Spotify.
- **Fonts added on single pages:** Abril Fatface (#KnowB4UNude), Special Elite (Online Harassment), Playfair Display italic (Vibe Check lockup).
- **Small content additions to confirm:** three Skills Lab tool rows on the Students page; a four-step row on the home page's Choking Hazard half; the six "exhibit" cards on Choking Hazard are reordered so each row carries similar text.
- **Not on the Skills Lab page:** "Take a Hint" and "Who's Right?" are not in the Skills Lab files I was given, so they are not listed.

**Carried over from 2.0 (still open)**

- Forms still don't submit anywhere. The tribute message, contact and program request forms open an email draft on a live site. Connect them to a form service before launch, the tribute wall first.
- The AI Literacy Training itself still describes Take It Down as "free removal of intimate images of anyone under 18"; this site uses Take It Down's own wording.
- The "about 20 pounds to open a can" figure on Choking Hazard is credited to Armstrong & Strack (2016) as on chokinghazard.org; the exact figure wasn't confirmed in the paper.
- Tattoo laws for minors come from a 2013 state-law review.
- Cover-up programs on Tattoos & Healing are news examples, not partners. Confirm they are still running.
- Everything on version 1's list (Peer Educator count, 15 lessons vs. 16 modules, six 360° components, Summer Institute name spellings, fundraiser dates).

## Source

`source/` holds the Python generator for 3.0 (one module per page group), the three briefs (`BRIEF.md`, `BRIEF2.md`, `BRIEF3.md`), `HANDOFF.md` (how to rebuild and what the rules are), the layout audit and screenshot tools, and the research notes behind the 2.0 pages. The generator's file paths point to the build workspace (`/home/claude/v3`), so update them before running it elsewhere, or edit the HTML in `site/` directly.
