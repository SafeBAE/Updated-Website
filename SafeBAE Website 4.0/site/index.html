Vibe Check by SafeBAE

Did I harm someone?Was that consent?Did I cross a boundary?Am I a bad person?Not sure if you crossed a line?

CheckYourVibe.org is a free, anonymous reflection tool for young people who are questioning whether they, or a friend, may have crossed a boundary or caused harm.

Young people are increasingly turning to anonymous forums and AI chatbots to ask difficult questions about relationships and consent. AI is built to tell you what you want to hear. Vibe Check guides users through confusing situations and connects them with real, evidence-based resources on consent, communication and accountability. No names, no accounts, and nothing is stored.

SafeBAE 360° Schools

SafeBAE’s whole-school approach: a comprehensive, survivor-centered prevention model rooted in trauma-informed care.

1

Peer Educator Training & Youth Leadership

Students complete a self-paced, online certification to become Peer Educators. Youth leaders help shape implementation, lead presentations, co-facilitate curriculum sessions, organize campaigns through SafeBAE Clubs, and receive ongoing mentorship from our team.

Learn more
2

Healthy Relationship & Consent Curriculum

A trauma-informed curriculum aligned with the National Health Education Standards. Lessons cover consent, boundaries, communication, digital safety, healing, and accountability, with lesson plans, slides, activities, and printable resources.

Learn more
3

Faculty & Staff Training

Professional development for teachers, staff, and administrators on trauma-informed response, supporting marginalized students, and building a school culture of consent and accountability. Flexible and tailored to each school.

Learn more
4

School Policy Support

Hands-on consultation to audit and revise sexual misconduct and Title IX policies, with annotated audits, student-centered model language, communication templates, and rollout strategies.

Learn more
5

Anonymous Reporting Tool (SafeBAE x REES)

A secure, private space where students can document incidents of harm at their own pace, report anonymously or by name to their school, and connect with school-specific resources and support.

Learn more
6

Educational Resources to Reduce Recidivism

Tools for students who have caused harm, supporting education, accountability, and behavior change. Includes the structured Accountability Training and Vibe Check, rooted in prevention, healing, and restorative justice rather than punishment.

Learn more

Schools can start with one component or build the whole model over time.

#ForDaisy

Remembering our co-founder, Daisy Coleman.

As a teenage survivor, Daisy shared her story in the documentary Audrie & Daisy. Her vision became the foundation of SafeBAE and helped ignite a youth-led movement. Every year, hundreds of people still find their way to this work through her story.

She was also a tattoo artist whose work people still carry with them.

All the organizing that happened after Daisy spoke out may not necessarily be a direct result but is certainly a ripple effect. The Cut, “What Daisy Coleman Changed”
