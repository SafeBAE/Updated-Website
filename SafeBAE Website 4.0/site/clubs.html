Start a SafeBAE club at your school

A SafeBAE club is a student-run chapter that brings consent and healthy relationship education to your school. You decide what your chapter does. We provide the training and materials, and our team is a quick email away when you need help. Registration is free.

Register your club ↗Already registered? Club Hub ↗
Registration is free Renews each school year

Three ways a club joins

Which one fits your school?

Start from scratch, restart a previous chapter, or add SafeBAE to a club you already run. The Club Hub walks you through each one step by step.

Bring SafeBAE to your school for the first time

Start a SafeBAE chapter from the ground up. Find an advisor, register your club, recruit your team, and use the Club Hub to plan meetings, activities, and events throughout the year.

All three register through the same form and get the same Hub access and support. Register below.

Bring SafeBAE into a club that’s already active

Add SafeBAE to the work your club is already doing. Register as an affiliate, get access to the Club Hub, and use our activities, campaigns, and resources as part of your existing programming.

All three register through the same form and get the same Hub access and support. Register below.

Bring your school’s SafeBAE chapter back

Pick up where a previous SafeBAE chapter left off. Re-register your chapter, reconnect with an advisor and students, and use the Club Hub to get meetings, activities, and events going again.

All three register through the same form and get the same Hub access and support. Register below.

Members only

Registering unlocks the Club Hub

The Club Hub is the members-only site for registered SafeBAE chapters. Access is free and renews with your registration each school year.

Inside, you’ll find

  • Find Your Next ActionFigure out what your chapter should do next.
  • Awareness Event LibraryEvent ideas, plus tools for planning your year.
  • Step-By-Step GuidesFor running meetings and organizing events.
  • Prevention Poster BuilderCreate posters with your school’s support resources.
  • Guides for Running Your ChapterFrom finding an advisor and recruiting members to getting media attention and preparing the next group of leaders.
  • And moreNew tools, activities, and resources are added to the Club Hub throughout the year.

You decide what your chapter does

Meetings, awareness events, campaigns, posters for your school: your chapter sets the plan. Members get the Club Hub, virtual training sessions with the SafeBAE team, and free materials.

After an event, send us photos and a couple of sentences. We share chapters’ work with clubs at other schools and on our social media, and we credit your club and school.
