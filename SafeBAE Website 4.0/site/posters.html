Warning posters on sexual strangulation

Choking Hazard is SafeBAE’s campaign on sexual strangulation, which young people call “choking”. Its free warning posters and warning sticker are available to download from the campaign site.

The film invites students to identify red flags, disrupt unhealthy patterns, and prevent toxic relationships.Watch and teach→PSA + teaching guide#knowB4UnudeOne of SafeBAE’s most iconic PSAs on the risks of sharing intimate images online.
