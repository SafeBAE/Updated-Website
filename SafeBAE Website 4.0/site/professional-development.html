Professional development for faculty & staff

Prevention is most effective when faculty and staff have the right tools and training. SafeBAE provides professional development for teachers, staff, and administrators that is trauma-informed, flexible, and tailored to your school’s needs.

Request a program→Book a 60-minute meeting ↗
  • For teachers, staff and administrators
  • Trauma-informed
  • In person or virtual
  • Tailored to your school

What the trainings cover

Every training is trauma-informed and built around your school. Sessions draw on these focus areas.

  • Trauma-informed responses to disclosures of harmRelated: Survivor support
  • Building a school culture of consent and accountabilityRelated: Consent Curriculum
  • Supporting student survivorsRelated: Title IX: rights at school
  • Understanding and advancing school policy reform to strengthen protections and reduce liabilityRelated: Policy Reform Guide
  • Supporting youth leadership and engagement in preventionRelated: Peer Educator Training
  • Supporting marginalized studentsRelated: Race, Rape Culture & Resistance

Pilot data · 2023–24 school year

What teachers reported

In collaboration with Dr. Lindsay Orchowski at Brown University, SafeBAE evaluated program delivery at two Rhode Island partner schools, Rhode Island Nurses Institute and Paul Cuffee Charter School, during the 2023⁠–⁠24 school year. Teachers who took part in SafeBAE’s programs were more likely to believe that their school policies could protect students from violence and thoroughly respond if violence occurs.

15%more teachers

believed students at their school would believe survivors of sexual violence after SafeBAE programming

9.4%more teachers

believed teachers and administrators would support survivors of sexual violence after SafeBAE programming

“[SafeBAE provides] information surrounding issues relevant to everyone, not just students.”
