## What is the Peer Educator Certification?

SafeBAE’s Certified Peer Educator Training is a free, self-paced training designed for high school students who want to lead sexual violence prevention in their schools and communities. Trauma-informed and accessible from anywhere, the program equips students to educate peers, challenge harmful norms, and build safer school environments.

The certification includes 16 interactive online modules, plus one-on-one support from SafeBAE’s team. Upon completion, students receive an official certification, along with access to ongoing leadership, organizing, and advocacy opportunities.

Peer educators become trusted voices in their schools — facilitating classroom discussions, leading school clubs, organizing events, running social media campaigns, and modeling what consent culture looks like in practice. This training creates a clear pathway for young people to drive cultural change.

## How It Works

**Becoming a Certified Peer Educator is simple, flexible, and fully supported. Students can register and complete the training entirely for free and at their own pace.**

#### 1. Complete the Registration Form

Fill out a short interest form to get started. We’ll send you everything you need to begin the training.

#### 2. Create Your Training Portal Account

Set up your secure account on Podia to unlock all 16 modules and additional resources.

#### 3. Complete 16 Modules

Learn about consent, healthy relationships, digital safety, and more across 16 interactive modules, completed at your own pace.

#### 4. Complete Post-Survey

Share feedback and reflect on what you’ve learned. Your responses help us improve the training and better support future Peer Educators.

#### 5. Earn Your Certificate

After completing all 16 modules and the post-training survey, you’ll receive your official SafeBAE Certified Peer Educator certificate.

#### 6. Start Leading

Put your skills into action by leading presentations, organizing campaigns, or starting a SafeBAE Club. You can also meet with the SafeBAE team to explore next steps.

## Why Peer Education Matters

Prevention only works when it feels real, and for most students, that means hearing it from someone they trust. Peer education brings authenticity, relatability, and lived experience into the conversation.

When youth lead, their peers listen differently. They ask more honest questions. They take ownership. They change behavior not because they were told to, but because they saw someone like them modeling something better.

*Learn more about the impact of youth-led prevention [here](https://www.safebae.org/resources/data-reports/).*

“As I went on in the training, I felt like I could be a more helpful and supportive person and that I would know what to do if something happened to me or anyone I know. I feel confident that I can help people in all sorts of situations and I am more educated on the effects of rape culture.”

— SafeBAE Certified Peer Educator
