## Justin's SafeBAE Fundraiser!

This month, I’m using my platform to raise awareness and funds for SafeBAE, a survivor-founded, youth-led organization dedicated to ending sexual violence and supporting mental health among middle and high school students. As a longtime educator, I believe every young person deserves to be in an environment free from sexual violence, harassment, and abuse.
