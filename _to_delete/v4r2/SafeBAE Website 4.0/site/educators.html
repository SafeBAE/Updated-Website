SafeBAE 360° Schools

SafeBAE 360° Schools is our whole-school approach: a comprehensive, survivor-centered model that builds lasting prevention infrastructure across your whole school community. It spans education, youth leadership, policy reform, and staff training, and it is flexible enough to start small and grow over time.

  6. Educational Resources to Reduce Recidivism
Introducing Vibe Check

CheckYourVibe.org

Vibe Check is a free, anonymous, self-guided tool that helps young people reflect on the health of their relationships. It is designed to be used privately and voluntarily.

It should never be assigned to students.

Making it available as an optional resource allows students who may need it to access it on their own terms, before you know anything specific about their situation. Consider sharing the link where students can find it without being asked to engage publicly:

  • In your syllabus
  • On a resource wall
  • Anywhere students can find it on their own
